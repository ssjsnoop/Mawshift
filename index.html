HOW TO PLAY

You are the experiment that got loose. Humans are armed. You are not impressed.

Move with the left thumb (drag anywhere on the left). ATTACK auto-aims at the nearest enemy.
Skills are the three round buttons. Locked skills open when you devour enough prey while in that form.
DEVOUR: fallen soldiers leave husks. Stand close and press DEVOUR to heal and level the form you are wearing.
FORM: shapeshift at any time. Time slows while you choose, and the change shoves enemies back.
Bosses: defeat one, then devour it to take its form.
Drones are machines. You cannot eat them.

PC: WASD move, Space/J or click attack, K / L / ; skills, E devour, 1-9 and 0 pick forms, Q next form, Esc pause.
